Probe calibration —

Anchors are taken from the last raw voltage the probe reported, not from a live read. Capture dry with the probe in air, and wet at field capacity: watered to runoff, then drained about 30 minutes. Capturing wet while it is still draining puts 100% above field capacity and every later reading looks drier than it is. These probes are inverted, so dry must be the HIGHER voltage; a swapped pair is refused outright. Both beds report from sleeping nodes, so wait for a fresh voltage before capturing.
